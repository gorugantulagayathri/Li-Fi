Focuses strictly on predictive blockage avoidance. Red walking triangles (library patrons) project an amber Predictive Warning Zone ahead of their travel trajectory. When the warning zone touches an active beam, the link turns ORANGE and executes a zero-downtime handover to a backup AP *before* the beam is physically cut.

Optical Beam Physics & Tri-State Color Logic

Unlike radio frequency (Wi-Fi) signals that pass through walls and humans with attenuation, Li-Fi uses visible light spectrum (400–700 THz). This delivers gigabit throughput and zero electromagnetic interference, but requires unoccluded optical Line-of-Sight (LoS).

Thick Green Beam

Active high-speed optical connection. Optical Line-of-Sight is unobstructed, transmitting high-throughput data at full transmission rate.

Thick Orange Beam (Pulsing)

Imminent threat warning. Moving obstacle's predictive warning zone has intersected the beam path. Triggers seamless proactive handover before physical occlusion.

Thin Red Beam

Blocked beam / network physical failure shadow. Occurs only if the physical obstacle cuts the beam and all backup APs are either blocked or at max capacity.
